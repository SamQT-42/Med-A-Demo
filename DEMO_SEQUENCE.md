# Med-A — demonstration sequence

The sequence from the brief: **explore a work difficulty → read a relevant
resource → inspect a support option → view a sample provider/vendor → save the
option or prepare a workplace request.**

Press **Reset demo** (foot of the sidebar) before you start. Roughly 90 seconds.

---

### 1 · Start with the difficulty, not the diagnosis (15s)

Open **Assistant**. Click **"Noise at work makes it hard to focus."**

> "The assistant asks one question at a time. It's scripted, and it says so on
> every message — we're not pretending there's a model behind this yet."

Click **Both**.

### 2 · Read a relevant resource (15s)

In the related panel on the right, everything the assistant mentioned is one
click away. Switch to **Library → Learn** and open **Working with distractions**.

> "Every card carries its source and a scope note. It's labelled a prototype
> summary based on public guidance — no reviewer, no endorsement, no quotations."

Click **Ask about this resource** to show it handing straight back to the assistant.

### 3 · Inspect a support option (20s)

**Library → Sensory pantry**. Open **Noise-reducing headphones**.

> "Descriptions are written around preference, never around a condition. Nothing
> here says 'recommended because you have ADHD'."

Point at the vendor block:

> "Example vendor, availability not connected. No price, no rating, no purchase."

Now open **A quieter place for focused work**.

> "This one is deliberately different — it's tagged 'workplace adjustment, not a
> product'. You can't buy it, so it has no vendor. It offers a draft instead."

### 4 · View a sample provider (20s)

**Library → Professional support**. Filter **Example area → North area** to show
filtering, then **Clear filters**.

Open **tamlyhoasung.com**.

> "These two are real organisations. We haven't verified their details, so rather
> than invent a service list, every unverified field says so and the card links
> to their own website. No phone numbers, no credentials, no claim of partnership."

Scroll to the sample enquiry:

> "A message you could adapt and send yourself. Med-A doesn't send it — there's
> no contact form and no recipient anywhere in this build."

### 5 · Save it, or prepare a request (20s)

Press **Save** on the listing. The sidebar count updates.

Back in the pantry, open **A quieter place for focused work** → **Draft a
support request**.

> "Editable, copyable, and it stays on this screen. Nothing simulates an employer
> receiving it or approving it."

Edit a word, click **Save to this session**, then open **Saved**.

> "Resources, listings, options and drafts all collect here. It's this browser tab
> only — no account, nothing stored anywhere. Reset demo clears it."

## If someone asks…

| Question | Answer, and where to click |
| --- | --- |
| "Does it diagnose?" | No. Assistant → *"Could my difficulties be related to ADHD?"* — it says it cannot determine that, and offers information, assessment options, or practical help. There's no questionnaire in the build. |
| "What if I just want practical help?" | Assistant → *"Show me sensory support options."* It says explicitly there's no need to discuss diagnosis first. |
| "What if I type something random?" | Type anything off-script. It says the prototype demonstrates a few guided conversations and re-offers the topics, rather than improvising. |
| "Can I talk to a person?" | Assistant → *"I would rather speak with a person."* It's honest that nobody is on the other end, and opens the directory instead. |
| "Are those real clinics?" | Yes — and their details are marked unverified, pending five minutes of the team's time. That's a deliberate choice, not an oversight. |

## Recovery

**Reset demo** at the foot of the sidebar returns everything to the opening
state in one click.
